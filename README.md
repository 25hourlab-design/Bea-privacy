# Privacy Policy for Bea

**Last updated: September 2026**

Bea is a cartomancy app developed by 25 Hour Lab.

## Information We Collect

Bea does not collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal details to use the app.

## Data Stored On Your Device

Bea stores the following information locally on your device only:
- Your daily reading count, to manage free and paid reading limits

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

## Payments

Payments are processed securely through Google Play In-App Billing. We do not store or have access to your payment information. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on how payment data is handled.

## Third Party Services

Bea uses the Anthropic Claude API to generate readings. Your questions and card selections are sent to Anthropic's servers to generate a response. Please refer to Anthropic's Privacy Policy at https://www.anthropic.com/privacy for more information.

## Children's Privacy

Bea is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Contact

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: hello@25hourlab.com

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
